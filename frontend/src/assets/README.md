# Community Illustration

`community-illustration.png` is original AI-generated concept artwork created on
24 September 2026 with Codex's built-in image-generation tool. An exact model
identifier was not exposed by the tool. The illustration depicts fictional characters and serves as an informal practice
example for the four workshop steps. It is not a validated research test stimulus.

Its generation prompts and editorial context are retained in the project's
community workshop concept pack under `docs/community-workshop-concept-2026-09-24`.
The workshop uses a neutral caption and descriptive alt text during Notice and Discuss. Its AI-generation source record is shown at Check. It does not reproduce
the reference flyer's institutional logos or inherit its no-AI statement.

No credential or watermark verification was performed on this asset.

On 24 September 2026 the built-in image tool corrected the older woman's missing
feet. The repaired image was visually reviewed and replaced in both this asset
directory and the concept pack. The full edit prompt and tool attribution are in
`Generation_Record.json` in that pack. Historical verification screenshots and
rollback copies remain unchanged; they are not served by the current app.

## Document previews

`infographic-preview.png` (3200 × 1787) and `presentation-preview.png` (3200 × 1800) show the complete first page/slide of the approved example originals. The PDF was rendered with Poppler; the PPTX was exported locally through LibreOffice and then rendered with Poppler on 27 September 2026. PowerPoint and the conference guide deck were not used.

The source documents contain raster artwork (the presentation's first image is 1376 × 768), so the larger renders preserve the available source detail rather than creating new detail. They replace the small UI previews, including the enlarged view after results open. SHA-256 matching in `exampleArtwork.ts` ties local input previews to their originals. The static demo selects covers by the approved bundle names, whose original hashes are checked at release preparation. Analysed-frame thumbnails and historical reports are preserved.
