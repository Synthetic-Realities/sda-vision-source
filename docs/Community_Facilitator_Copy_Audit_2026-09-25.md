# Community and Facilitator presentation

## Activity layout

Community Workshop and the illustrated facilitator guide follow four activities: Notice, Discuss,
Check and Reflect. The opening illustration and the ten menu examples have saved
findings in the public demo. The [workshop guide](Workshop_Activities_2026-09-25.md)
records their use and session behaviour.

| Surface | Presentation |
| --- | --- |
| Start here | A large, bold, black heading above the example selector. |
| Notice | Optional first impressions and a note. |
| Discuss | Media-specific visual, sound or text cues and a conversation note. |
| Check | A compact findings overview, provider evidence and recorded-analysis action. |
| Reflect | First and later views, practical next actions and a reflection note. |
| Facilitator guide (PDF) | Illustrated group prompts and session guidance; participants can pass or revise a view. |
| Provider evidence and ratings | File history, Content Credentials, validation/trust context and full research results. |
| About/privacy and session reset | Storage, response lifetime and download guidance. |

## Evidence and source information

Personal impressions and automated assessments have distinct labels. Selecting
Made with AI records a participant's view. Source records and credential findings
have their own evidence and scope. C2PA presence, integrity, signer trust and
declared origin remain separate statuses; failed and incomplete checks retain
their recorded status.

The evidence box offers original-file downloads, Google Lens/image-search
information and Second Opinion controls where relevant. Google and vendor links
open their own services; the visitor chooses any external attachment. The public
demo holds pasted replies in the browser and offers session downloads. Research
mode's optional note-scoring action has its own recipient and processing disclosure.

The provider table distinguishes completed, disabled, failed and unavailable
assessments. Ratings are not calibrated probabilities. Audio observations,
transcript assessments and credential declarations retain their specific scope.
