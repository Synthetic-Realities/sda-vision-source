## Abstract Draft For Researcher Review

**Working title: SDA Vision: exploring evidence and uncertainty in AI-generated and traditional media**

**Dr Sam Martin (she/her)** — Smart Data Research UK (UKRI) Fellow (Grant number UKRI4010.),
Manchester Metropolitan University (MMU).

SDA Vision supports qualitative researchers and workshop participants in examining
how AI-generated and traditional media may have been produced. The application presents visual model
assessments, content credential findings and supporting observations alongside
their evidence, coverage and limitations. Images, documents and sampled video
frames can be explored individually or in prepared batches. Community Workshop
and the illustrated facilitator guide organise discussion around Notice, Discuss, Check and
Reflect. Participants can compare findings, consider source information and
download reports. Second Opinion replies from external services remain separate
from the combined assessment. Ratings are not calibrated probabilities, and
disagreement or missing credentials leaves questions for review. The
public interactive demonstration uses prepared examples and saved results, while
a presenter-operated conference profile supports fresh analyses of selected
originals. This demonstration invites discussion about how evidence-aware media
literacy can support engagement with health information, with attention
to the researcher's role in interpreting findings and deciding what to check next.
