## What Changed

| Area | Updated approach | Boundary retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Header and results | Compare findings; concise outcome headings | Inconclusive stays inconclusive; ratings are not calibrated probabilities |
| Additional findings | Neutral headings and record-based empty states | Disagreement and actual provider statuses remain visible |
| Audio | Coverage once, clearer excerpt and vendor rows | Track presence, content description and origin are separate |
| Consent | Recipient and material in first-person agreement wording | Google excerpt limit/account/charges; Suno complete original including metadata |
| External opinions | Shorter instructions; record the check status with the reply | Tool errors remain failed checks; note scoring goes to Anthropic only on action |
| Source checks | Plain-language source labels and researcher attribution | Required fields, deletion confirmation and score isolation unchanged |
| C2PA | Separate integrity, trust, declarations and coverage | Failed/incomplete/untrusted results retain their actual status and restrictions |
| Graphs | Analysis relationships and unscored context | Distribution history remains unassessed; acoustic AI detection remains outside scope |
| Exports | Matching headings and self-contained scope statements | Structured fields and original recorded evidence remain intact |
| Privacy | Factual retention and provider-setting guidance | Local reports remain unencrypted until deletion; existing cleanup caveats remain |

## Deliberately Unchanged

- Provider prompts, copied second-opinion prompt, model identifiers, scores,
  thresholds, verdict enums, trust policy and API request/response schemas.
- Consent behaviour, hash binding, optional upload limits, provider recipients,
  retention settings, temporary-file handling and `.env`.
- Raw provider/vendor replies, researcher-pasted notes, historical reports and
  frozen showcase builds. Those records may still contain earlier wording.
- Institutional approval records, licensing terms and publication release gates.

Current exports use the revised headings and scope text while preserving the
wording recorded in the report's evidence, explanations and notes. No display-time
substitution rewrites historical findings. A fresh analysis uses the new app-owned
result prose. Existing open browser results may retain their earlier text.
