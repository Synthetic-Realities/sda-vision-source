# SDA Vision: Release Notes Draft

Local review revision: 15 October 2026. Software 0.1.3-rc.1; guide resources
1.7 review. Publication versions/date await researcher confirmation.

## Participant Workflow

- SynthID Detector and the OpenAI image checker are shown side by side in the
  recorded demo, with a simple paste-results box under each. They stack on
  narrow screens.
- The former Gemini chat shortcut and detailed file-identity form are removed
  from the participant steps. The separate research interface retains its
  structured workflow.
- The demo uses prepared examples and page/slide images. OpenAI's shortcut is
  enabled for image examples. SynthID notes retain the selected-material context.
- Session downloads include the pasted replies separately from saved model
  findings. Notes are held in browser memory; download them before leaving,
  reloading or changing examples.

## Facilitator Resources

The eight-page guide and one-page participant sheet describe Open, Paste and
Keep, with an updated screenshot, recipient/account disclosures and an option
to follow the facilitator or discuss a previously recorded reply. Matching
Markdown instructions and the web guide are included.

## Packaging

The local source archive contains a newly built Research frontend, tests,
selected original examples, guide resources and dependency notices. A separate
static archive preserves the recorded demo and its saved findings. New guide
assets and game artwork/notice have been added to the explicit source allowlist.
File-level checksums accompany the review pack.

## Scope

This revision changes presentation and documentation. Provider prompts, models,
aggregation, trust policy, structured research schemas and scientific method
versions are unchanged. Historical reports and cached graphs retain their
recorded bytes; pasted notes do not become independent visual-model votes.
Ratings are not calibrated probabilities. AI-origin detection from the sound
itself is outside this workflow. Graph distribution history is not assessed.

No new live model assessment, external media upload, hosting deployment or
remote publication was performed. Live service availability, physical-device
acceptance and release permissions remain review items.
