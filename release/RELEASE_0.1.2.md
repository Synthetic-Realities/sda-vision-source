# SDA Vision 0.1.2

Interface and workshop-resource release · 6 October 2026.

The approved NotebookLM podcast has exploratory prompts in Notice, Discuss, Check and Reflect. Check offers a Google text search using the cover's authors and topic, plus a collapsed source explanation with the Vaccine paper link. Both links open in a new tab. The prompts distinguish podcast production, its research source and the fidelity of the adaptation. Source details enter the session summary after the participant opens the reveal.

The final, source-informed workshop verdict is **Mix of human and AI**: human-authored research, selected by Dr Sam Martin and adapted into a podcast with Google NotebookLM. Following the cover citation, finding the publication and comparing a podcast statement with the paper helps the group reach a fuller Human–AI reflection. The recorded transcript ratings and separate audio-excerpt description remain available with their original scope; they inform the discussion alongside the additional source evidence.

Session exports use the displayed cover, first slide or still for audio, documents, presentations and video, with a recorded-thumbnail fallback. Image exports retain the selected original. Preview captions distinguish the illustration from the material assessed. Google Lens remains limited to image originals.

The changes are shared by the demo, local Research/Conference interface and installation pack. Provider prompts, ratings, agreement rules, credential policy, original media and saved reports retain their existing content and method versions. Software 0.1.2 identifies this interface release. The companion facilitator guide is version 1.5 and includes the podcast activity on page 6.
