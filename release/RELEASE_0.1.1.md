# SDA Vision 0.1.1

Software maintenance release · 29 September 2026.

Community Workshop's Reflect step starts with the recorded findings and Evidence
box collapsed together under **Revisit the recorded findings**. The reflection
questions, **Download session notes** and **New session** remain visible. Opening
the review restores access to the evidence; notes are retained between Check and Reflect.

Google Lens is available when the selected original is an image. PDF documents,
PowerPoint presentations, podcasts and videos keep their original-file download
controls and use the checks appropriate to their media type.

Both behaviours are shared by the public saved-results demo, local Research and
Conference profiles, workshop build and installable source. Provider prompts,
agreement rules, credential trust policy, recorded findings and original media
retain their existing versions and contents. Software version 0.1.1 identifies
this interface release; saved analysis records retain the version that made them.

The public-source packaging allowlist refers to the files shipped in the public
repository, including its current guide. Missing required inputs and unsafe
symlinks continue to stop packaging.
